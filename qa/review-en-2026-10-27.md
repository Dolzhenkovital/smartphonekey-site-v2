# Independent review: SmartphoneKey website 2.0 (English)

Date: 27 October 2026. Reviewer: independent agent (did not build the site).
Instance: local Odoo 18 at http://127.0.0.1:8069, `preview_mode` on (40 px banner, every page `noindex, nofollow`).
Method: headless Chrome via puppeteer-core (new browser context per page, `Accept-Language: en`), Python `urllib` fetch of all 27 responses for structural checks, baseline images in `qa/baseline-45b3296/` and baseline CSS read from git revision 45b3296 (`git show`, read-only). No files in the addon were changed.
Screenshots: `implementation/qa/review-en/` (file names are cited per finding). The first-pass `focus-*.png` crops of elements below the first viewport are mis-clipped; use `focus2-*.png` and `focus-contact-sheet-2.png` for focus states.

Severity: **Blocking** = must not ship; **Should fix** = fix before the owner review or launch; **Minor** = polish or documentation.

Totals: **0 Blocking, 11 Should fix, 30 Minor** (41 findings, numbered F1–F41).

Environment limits that affect the verdict:
- `/guide-for-usage-and-installation` and `/instructions/*` return **404** in this instance (served by the `smartphonekey` module, which is not installed here). Every header "Instructions" link, five footer links and the only filled button on `/platform/access-management` point there (F41).
- hreflang is not emitted in preview mode by design (`rendering.page_values`: alternates only when not noindex), so the 5 + x-default set could not be observed (F38).

## 1. Agent comprehension check (home, first screen only, no scrolling)

*This is an agent check, not a user study.* What follows is what an agent reading only the first screen concludes.

### 1280×800 (`home-1280x800.jpg`)

Visible: H1 "Manage access across your properties in one place", the lead, a filled "Explore the platform" button, a "Discuss your project" link, a reader photo on a tinted panel with a four-line caption, and the three-part strip "Admin Panel › Ways to enter › Reader at the door" (y 666–768). The header shows Instructions, Admin Panel and an outlined "Site recommendations".

- **Product category:** software for controlling who can open doors in buildings, plus SmartphoneKey's own door reader. The exact category ("access management platform") is never named on this screen. "Platform" appears only inside a button label.
- **Who it is for:** whoever runs residential or mixed properties: owners, property managers, site administrators. The cue is "residents, staff and contractors".
- **Main benefit:** one place to decide and change who gets in, across several properties, and to see what happened.
- **Next action:** "Explore the platform" (filled). The alternatives are "Discuss your project" (a link) and "Site recommendations" (an outlined header button). Existing users get Instructions and Admin Panel.
- **Role of the reader:** the device at the door. It reads cards and digital keys, contains the access controller, and runs on LTE or Wi-Fi. It is one part of a three-part system, not the product itself.
- **Main versus secondary features:** the main features are giving and changing access, cards and digital keys, events and device status. No secondary feature appears on this screen, and card programming is absent (correct).
- **What was unclear:**
  - "Manage access" could mean IT identity management until the eye reaches the photo or the strip. The word "door" first appears in the strip at y 681.
  - The strip lists "the SmartphoneKey app" under "Ways to enter", which reads as if the app opens doors (see F3).
  - The photo caption is a disclaimer ("it does not show whether it is the LTE or the Wi-Fi version") and adds nothing to understanding.
  - Two teal actions compete: the filled "Explore the platform" and the outlined header "Site recommendations".

### 390×844 (`home-390x844.jpg`)

Visible: banner, logo, Menu, a three-line H1, a four-line lead, the button at y 455–503, the link at y 507–551, and the top of the photo from y≈575 (cut).

- **Category, audience, benefit:** the same reading as on desktop, but it rests on the H1 and lead alone. Neither "door" nor "platform" (except in the button) is visible. The photo is cut, without its caption.
- **Next action:** clear ("Explore the platform").
- **Role of the reader:** not explained on this screen, because the strip starts at about 1,000 px. All that is visible is a black device with a hand-and-phone icon.
- **What was unclear:** whether this is software, hardware or both. Existing-user routes are hidden behind Menu.

**F1 · Should fix · `views/home.xml`.** Neither size names the category or the door on the first screen. The brief asks the site to "explain the product (access management platform; readers are part of it)". Expected: the lead says it, for example "SmartphoneKey is an access management platform for buildings: give residents, staff and contractors access to the doors they need…" (the deck lead would need the same change).

**F2 · Minor · `views/home.xml`.** The hero caption takes four lines of 13 px muted text (`home-1280x800.jpg`, y 552–625): "SmartphoneKey reader with backplate. Product image from the installation guide; it does not show whether it is the LTE or the Wi-Fi version." Expected: "SmartphoneKey reader" in the hero, with the LTE/Wi-Fi note kept on `/products/readers`.

## 3. Copy and product facts

Text checked: the main text of all 27 responses, plus titles and descriptions.

**Clean:**
- No third-party programmer or programming-device implication. "Third-party card programmer" appears only as the technical answer on `/compatibility` and in the MIFARE guide, exactly as the deck allows.
- No EV1/EV2, "any Matter device", "unlimited", "generally available", prices, demo gating or range figures. "Failover" appears only in negations.

- The lead says: "Residents, staff and visitors enter with a digital key, a card or the entrance QR code."
- The QR code is a contact path (claim C16 "Explain guest scan/contact workflow"; the home page says "Visitors scan the code at the entrance to contact a resident or the site team"). As written, the sentence says visitors open doors with the QR code.
