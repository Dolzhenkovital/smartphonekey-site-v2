Denys Merkulov

CTO

Denys Merkulov brings extensive expertise in overseeing technical strategies and innovation within the telecom and technology sectors. With a proven track record in leading complex projects and production initiatives from inception through implementation, Denys has successfully established and optimized high-performance production facilities in key markets such as China and Ukraine.

In his role as CTO at SmartphoneKey, Denys drives the technological vision, ensuring the integration of cutting-edge solutions across product development, manufacturing processes, and logistics operations. He is committed to enhancing operational efficiency, scalability, and quality assurance, laying the foundation for sustainable growth and technological excellence. Denys continuously seeks innovative methods to elevate operational performance, solidifying SmartphoneKey's position as a leader in smart access technology.
