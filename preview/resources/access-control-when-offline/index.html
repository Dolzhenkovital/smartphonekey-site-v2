What happens when a reader goes offline?

By the SmartphoneKey product team

Published 27 October 2026

Updated 27 October 2026

Product facts checked 26 October 2026

Some changes you make in the Admin Panel wait until the reader reconnects. A pending change has not reached the reader yet, so a revocation made while a reader is offline is not in effect there until the reader confirms it. Whether keys and cards keep working at the door during an outage depends on the device and its configuration. Check the status of a change before you rely on it.

Four states of a change

States of a key change
StateWhat it meansWhat to do
RequestedThe change was made in the Admin Panel.Follow its status.
Pending deliveryThe change is waiting for the reader to reconnect. It is not on the reader yet.Treat the previous access as still possible on that reader.
Confirmed by the readerThe reader reported that it applied the change.For an unlock, this shows the reader acted. It does not prove a person passed through or the door opened.
Failed or unconfirmedThe reader reported an error, or no confirmation arrived.Check the reader and its connection, then repeat the change or contact support.

Not every kind of change waits in the queue: some need the reader to be online when you make them.

What this means for revocation

A revocation made while a reader is offline is pending, not enforced. Until the reader reconnects and confirms the change, the key or card may still work on that reader. When access must end at once, restore the reader's connection, watch for the confirmation and follow your site's own procedures in the meantime.

What an administrator should check

  • The reader's connection status.
  • Pending changes for the person and the doors involved.
  • The confirmation once the reader is back online.
  • Device alerts, if you have configured them.

Local access and other services

Whether keys and cards keep working at the door during an outage depends on the device and its configuration. Other services rely on the connection in their own ways: the QR intercom, video, AI Concierge and issuing new keys or cards do not share one outage behaviour, so check each one you depend on.

Recovery and support

When the reader reconnects, queued changes are delivered and their status updates. If a change stays unconfirmed, contact support with the site and reader details. Please do not send passwords or access codes by email.
