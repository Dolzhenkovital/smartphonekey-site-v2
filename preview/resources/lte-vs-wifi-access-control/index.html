LTE or Wi-Fi: what fits your site?

By the SmartphoneKey product team

Published 27 October 2026

Product facts checked 26 October 2026

LTE and Wi-Fi are connection choices. The right choice depends on coverage at the planned reader location, network ownership and the operating requirements of the site. Start with those constraints before comparing reader variants. Both still need power, compatible lock hardware and a careful installation: the connection only decides how the reader reaches the platform.

Compare the two connections

The same requirements apply to every SmartphoneKey reader. What changes is the connection each one depends on.

LTE and Wi-Fi reader requirements
RequirementLTE readerWi-Fi reader
Internet connectionCellular coverage and suitable SIM and serviceSuitable Wi-Fi coverage and network configuration
Site assessmentCarrier, region, signal and installation positionCoverage at the reader, access details and network requirements
Best starting pointSites where cellular connectivity fits the deploymentSites with suitable managed Wi-Fi available
Physical installationPower and compatible door hardware requiredPower and compatible door hardware required
Thread rolesThread Border Router and mesh router, no separate firmwareThread Border Router and mesh router, no separate firmware
Card programmingMIFARE Classic and DESFire EV3 workflows; confirm the model and firmwareMIFARE Classic and DESFire EV3 workflows; confirm the model and firmware

Who manages the connection

With an LTE reader, the reader relies on a cellular service. Someone has to provide the SIM and service, keep it active and check coverage at the position where the reader will be mounted.

With a Wi-Fi reader, the reader joins the site's own network. The network owner provides the access details, keeps the network configured as the installation guide describes, and tells you before passwords or equipment change.

Power and lock hardware are still needed

The connection does not change the physical installation. Each reader still needs appropriate power, compatible locking hardware and the connections specified in the installation and connection guide.

Checklist before you choose

For an LTE reader

  • Cellular coverage measured at each planned reader position, not just outside the building.
  • Who supplies the SIM and service, and in which region the reader is installed.
  • Who renews the service and who is told if it stops.

For a Wi-Fi reader

  • Wi-Fi coverage at each planned reader position.
  • Who owns and manages the network, and whether it restricts new devices.
  • How you will learn about changes to the network name, password or equipment.

When to ask for a site review

Ask us to review the site when coverage at a reader position is uncertain, when doors are in basements or behind heavy construction, when several buildings share one plan, or when a third party manages the network. Coverage depends on the building, placement and radio conditions, so it is checked on site rather than promised in advance.

Choose the connection that fits the site, then confirm the supported reader configuration.
