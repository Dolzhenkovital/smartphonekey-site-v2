Access management for buildings

Manage access across your properties in one place

Give residents, staff and contractors access to the doors they need. Manage Wallet keys and cards, update permissions and see device activity in SmartphoneKey.

Explore the platform Discuss your project

What SmartphoneKey gives you

  • Manage access centrally

    Add people, choose their doors and update access across your sites.

  • Enter with a Wallet key or card

    Offer Apple Wallet and Google Wallet keys on supported devices, alongside physical NFC cards.

    • Apple Wallet
    • Google Wallet
    • NFC card

  • Plan the connection for each door

    Combine readers with a built-in access controller and LTE, Wi-Fi or supported Thread connectivity.

From installation to everyday access

  1. Install

    An installer connects the reader, power and compatible lock hardware, then configures the connection.

  2. Grant access

    An administrator selects the doors and assigns access to the right person.

  3. Enter

    The key holder uses a supported Wallet key or NFC card at an authorised door.

See how the platform works

Access that changes with your building

A new resident moves in
Assign a card or supported digital key for their doors and shared areas.
A contractor is visiting
Set a validity period for supported guest access.
An employee needs another door
Update their permissions as responsibilities change.
Someone leaves
Revoke their access and check that the readers have applied the change.

Changes for an offline reader remain pending until it reconnects and applies them.

Help inside the Admin Panel

Get help in the Admin Panel with the built-in AI Assistant. Available actions depend on your permissions and site configuration.

Explore access management

A reader and access controller in one device

Choose connectivity to suit the building. SmartphoneKey readers can use LTE or Wi-Fi, and can participate in a Thread mesh with a connected reader providing internet access.

A suitable wireless design can reduce communication cabling between readers.

Power, compatible lock hardware and local wiring are still required. Network layout and coverage depend on the installation.
