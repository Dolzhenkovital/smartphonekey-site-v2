Handle everyday access changes

Make the change in the Admin Panel and check that the reader has applied it.

  2. Change access

Keys, cards and visitors

Residents and staff enter with a Wallet key or an NFC card. Visitors use the entrance QR code to reach someone. Key holders manage their keys in the SmartphoneKey app.

Wallet key
A digital key in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. The person holds a supported phone to the reader.
NFC card
A physical card for people who prefer one or do not use a phone to enter.
Visitor QR code
Visitors scan the code at the entrance to contact a resident or the site team.
SmartphoneKey app
Where key holders manage their keys, on Android and iPhone. Entry at the door uses a Wallet key or a card.
