Assess the security of your installation

Security depends on the whole installation: who can change access, the readers and their network, and how the site is operated. Here is what SmartphoneKey controls and shows, and which documents to check.

Discuss technical requirements View certifications

Controls

Permissions by site

Administrators work only on the sites they are given. What each person can see and do follows their permissions and the services configured for the account.

Changes you can check

Events and change status show whether a change was requested, confirmed by the reader or failed. A change waiting for an offline reader is not yet in effect there.

Devices and networks

Check the capabilities and network roles of each device. A working connection does not certify the whole installation, and alerts are not a response-time guarantee.

Product documents

Open the regulatory records and certificates for the readers. Each document applies only to the model and scope it names.

View certifications

Questions for procurement

Ask about the deployment architecture, responsibilities, data retention, access permissions and the documents your project requires. We answer for your configuration rather than assume one setup fits every requirement.
