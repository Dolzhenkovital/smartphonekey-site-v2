Manage who can open which doors

Add people to a site, give them a card or digital key, change or revoke their access, and check that each reader has applied the change.

Read the Admin Panel guide Discuss your project

Example: a contractor's visit

  1. Choose the site and the doors
  2. Issue a key or card
  3. Check that the readers confirmed it
  4. Revoke when the work ends, and check again

What you can do

People and their access

Add residents, staff and contractors to the site they belong to and choose the doors they can open. For any site, review its people, keys, cards, readers and related devices.

Site administrators

Give a local administrator the sites they look after. What each person can see and do depends on their permissions and your subscription.

Cards and digital keys

Issue Wallet keys, manage NFC cards and give temporary access where it is supported. When access is no longer needed, revoke it and check that each reader has applied the change.

Example: a contractor's visit

A contractor needs access to a service room. The administrator chooses the site and the doors, issues a key or card, and checks that the readers have confirmed it. When the work ends, the administrator revokes the access and checks again.

Events and device status

Review activity and audit records for each site. Device monitoring shows readers that have lost their connection and operations that failed. You can configure alerts for supported device events; they do not replace an agreed support process.

When a reader is offline

Some changes wait until the reader reconnects. Pending means the reader has not confirmed the change yet. Whether keys and cards keep working at the door during an outage depends on the device and its configuration.

A revocation made while the reader is offline is not in effect on that reader until it confirms the change.

Questions

Can I manage more than one site?

Yes. Each site is managed on its own, and you can give administrators access to the sites they look after. The actions available depend on account permissions and configured services.

What happens to a key change while a reader is offline?

Some changes stay pending until the reader reconnects. Check the status: a revocation that is still pending is not yet in effect on that reader.
