Bring access management into one clear system.

SmartphoneKey connects the work of managing sites, people, credentials and supported devices. The Admin Panel is the place to organise that work; readers and connected devices carry it into the building.

Start with the site.

Group the people and devices that belong to a customer installation. Use the site's context to review access and assign local management responsibilities.

Each site is organised as an Entity in the Admin Panel.

Manage the whole access lifecycle.

Add a person, assign the required credentials, review activity and change access when their needs change. For a disconnected device, some changes may remain pending until it reconnects.

  1. Add a personto the site they belong to
  2. Assign credentialswallet, card or supported temporary access
  3. Review activitysee what is pending and what is confirmed
  4. Change or revokeand follow the delivery status

Connect the physical and the digital.

SmartphoneKey readers provide the door-side hardware. Supported cards and digital credentials provide ways to enter. Hubs and Matter devices extend the connected-device workflow.

Keep the result understandable.

A request being accepted is not the same as a device confirming its action. The interface separates these states so operators can see what still needs attention.

  • Available screens and actions depend on permissions and configured services.
  • Card programming depends on the card family and the programming device.
  • Device workflows depend on the capabilities each device reports.

Questions

What is SmartphoneKey?

SmartphoneKey is an access control management platform. It combines the Admin Panel, supported credentials, SmartphoneKey readers and connected-device workflows to help teams manage access across their sites.

Is SmartphoneKey only a reader?

The readers are part of the system. The platform also provides site, user and credential management, supported device workflows and operational visibility.

Do I need a demo to learn more?

No. Explore the platform, check compatibility and use the setup guide without sharing contact details. Contact the team when you want a project discussion, technical answer, partnership conversation or walkthrough.
