How SmartphoneKey works

SmartphoneKey is an access management platform. Administrators manage sites and access in the Admin Panel. Residents and staff enter with a digital key or a card; visitors scan the entrance QR code to contact a resident or the site team. Readers at the doors apply each change and report what happened.

Organised by site

Each building or location is a site. A site holds its doors and readers, the people who use them and their access, so you can work on one site or review many.

Sites are called Entities in the Admin Panel.

Administrators and key holders

Administrators make the changes; key holders use the result at the door.

  1. Add a person to the site they belong to
  2. Issue access a Wallet key, an NFC card or temporary access where supported
  3. Check the status pending, confirmed or failed
  4. Change or revoke when their needs change or they leave

Key holders use a Wallet key on a supported phone or an NFC card at the door, and can manage their keys in the SmartphoneKey app. Visitors scan the QR code at the entrance.

What makes up the system

The Admin Panel is where you manage access. Readers at the doors apply it. Hubs and supported Matter devices add connected-device scenarios.

Know when a change has reached the door

Sending a change is not the same as a reader applying it. The Admin Panel shows whether each change is pending, confirmed by the reader or failed, and which readers are online, offline or not connected. A revocation waiting for an offline reader is not yet in effect there.

  • What each person can see and do depends on their permissions and the services on your account.
  • Scenarios can use only the capabilities each device reports.

Questions

What is SmartphoneKey?

SmartphoneKey is an access management platform. It combines the Admin Panel, Wallet keys and NFC cards, SmartphoneKey readers with a built-in access controller, and connected-device scenarios, so teams can manage access across their sites.

Is SmartphoneKey only a reader?

No. The readers are part of the system. The platform also covers sites, people and their access, cards and digital keys, events and device status.

What does a resident need to enter?

Whatever the site issues to them: a Wallet key on a supported phone, or an NFC card. They can manage their keys in the SmartphoneKey app on Android or iPhone. Visitors use the QR code at the entrance.
