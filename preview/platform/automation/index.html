Turn connected devices into practical workflows.

Use the Scenario Builder to combine supported triggers, conditions and actions. Start with a simple rule or build a more detailed workflow from the capabilities your devices report.

Four examples.

Each one is an example of what supported devices can do together, not a promise for every device.

Example scenarios
ExampleWhenOnly ifThen
Light the entranceA compatible contact sensor reports an open doorDuring a configured evening scheduleTurn on a supported light
Respond to a leakA compatible leak sensor reports waterAlwaysSend a close command to a supported water valve. Confirm the device response and retain manual control.
Adjust the roomA supported temperature sensor reaches a chosen thresholdAlwaysChange a compatible thermostat's setpoint
Close the working dayA scheduleSupported conditions you chooseSwitch selected lights or sockets. Keep essential equipment outside this rule.

Check the capabilities behind the example.

A Matter label does not mean every device exposes the same commands. Scenarios depend on the commissioned devices, their reported capabilities and firmware. In the current implementation, each scenario belongs to one hub.

Questions

Does SmartphoneKey work with every Matter device?

Compatibility depends on the device type, exposed capabilities, firmware and configuration. Check the functions required by your workflow instead of assuming every Matter device supports them.
