What a scenario can use

A Matter label does not mean every device offers the same functions. A scenario can use only what each commissioned device reports and its firmware supports. Each scenario runs on one hub and the devices commissioned to it.

See supported device capabilities

Check that the hub applied the change

The Admin Panel shows whether a scenario change is waiting for the hub, applied or rejected. Until the hub applies an edit, the version already on the hub keeps running.

Questions

Does SmartphoneKey work with every Matter device?

It depends on the device type, the capabilities it reports, its firmware and your configuration. Check the functions your scenario needs rather than assuming every Matter device offers them.

Can one scenario use devices on two hubs?

No. Each scenario currently belongs to one hub and the devices commissioned to it. Plan each scenario around the hub that controls the devices it needs.
