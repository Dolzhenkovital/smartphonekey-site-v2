Connect visitors with the right person.

With the QR intercom workflow, a visitor scans the code at the entrance and follows the site's configured contact path. Residents and site teams can manage visitor access within their available permissions and services.

From the entrance to an answer.

  1. Scanthe QR code at the entrance
  2. Followthe guest instructions
  3. Contactthe resident or available service
  4. Receivea clear outcome

Explore AI Concierge for your site.

AI Concierge is designed to help interpret a visitor's request and work with configured access rules. Ask about the version and availability appropriate to your site.

AI Concierge features and rollout status vary by deployment. Confirm availability and the required setup with our team.

Keep permission separate from conversation.

The v2 design uses AI to interpret the request and server-side rules to decide whether it can proceed. Do not interpret a convincing conversation as a permission grant.

If you cannot complete the guest flow, use the site's provided resident or support contact. The marketing site is not an emergency service or a live door-control interface.
