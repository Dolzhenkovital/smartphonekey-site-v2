How a visit works

  1. Scan

    the QR code at the entrance

  2. Follow

    the instructions on the phone

  3. Reach

    the resident or the site's service

  4. Get

    a clear answer

AI Concierge: ask about availability

AI Concierge is designed to understand what a visitor asks for and check it against the site's access rules. Ask about the version and availability for your site.

AI Concierge features and rollout status vary by deployment. Confirm availability and the required setup with our team.

Access rules decide, not the conversation

AI Concierge uses AI to understand the request. Access rules on the server decide whether it can go ahead, so a persuasive conversation does not grant access.

If a visitor cannot complete the steps, they use the resident or support contact the site provides. This website is not an emergency service and cannot open doors.
