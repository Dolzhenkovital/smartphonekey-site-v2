Compatibility depends on the reader revision, firmware, credential format and device capabilities. Use the guidance below to start the check; confirm the configuration for your project before ordering.

Physical card programming.

Documented workflows: MIFARE Classic and MIFARE DESFire EV3. A family name is not a guarantee for every memory size, credential format or third-party card. Reading a card identifier and provisioning a credential are different operations.

Physical card programming
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
MIFARE Classic Program Credential programming through the Admin Panel workflow with a compatible SmartphoneKey programming device, including the reader's built-in programmer where available. Confirm the card's memory size and credential format before ordering. Reading a card identifier is a different operation from provisioning a credential.
MIFARE DESFire EV3 Program Credential programming through the Admin Panel workflow with a compatible SmartphoneKey programming device, including the reader's built-in programmer where available. The documented workflow names DESFire EV3. Other DESFire generations and memory sizes need confirmation before they are planned. Manufacturer or standard information

Digital credentials.

Supported wallet and mobile-access workflows depend on the device, platform and account configuration. Confirm the phone, operating system and credential requirements; do not assume power-reserve behaviour for every phone.

Digital credentials
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
Wallet credentials Manage Issue and revoke supported wallet credentials for people and doors from the Admin Panel. Depends on the phone, its operating system and the account configuration. Do not assume the same behaviour when a phone's battery is low or empty.
Physical NFC keys Manage Assign, review and revoke physical NFC keys alongside digital credentials. Programming a new card needs a supported card family and programming device.

Matter devices.

Support is capability-based. A device may expose on/off, contact, temperature, lock, valve or other capabilities; available operations depend on what it reports and what the configured system supports. Specific third-party models appear only after compatibility testing.

Matter devices
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
Matter devices, by capability Control Devices that report supported capabilities, such as on/off, contact, temperature, lock or valve, can be commissioned and used in device management and scenarios. Available operations depend on what the device reports, its firmware and the configured system. Commissioning a device does not prove every command works. Specific third-party models are listed only after compatibility testing. Manufacturer or standard information
Scenario Builder Control Simple and advanced scenarios combine supported triggers, conditions and actions. Each current scenario belongs to one hub and its commissioned devices. An edited scenario replaces the version on the hub only once the hub has applied it.

Networks.

Thread mesh participation, Border Router functions, Matter control and internet uplink are separate roles. Confirm which roles the proposed hardware and firmware provide.

Networks
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
LTE reader connection Connect Internet connection over cellular service. Needs cellular coverage at the reader position and a suitable SIM and service. No automatic switch to Wi-Fi is implied.
Wi-Fi reader connection Connect Internet connection over the site's Wi-Fi network. Needs suitable Wi-Fi coverage at the reader position and the network configuration given in the installation guide.
Reader through a Matter hub Connect Supported reader configurations can connect through a Matter hub. Depends on the reader model and firmware. This is not a radio-range or failover guarantee.

Questions

Which physical cards can be programmed?

The documented workflows support MIFARE Classic and MIFARE DESFire EV3 using compatible programming hardware and firmware. Confirm the exact card variant and credential format before ordering.

Does SmartphoneKey work with every Matter device?

Compatibility depends on the device type, exposed capabilities, firmware and configuration. Check the functions required by your workflow instead of assuming every Matter device supports them.

Need a specific check?

Tell us the reader model, firmware and device or card details. Do not send keys, passwords or credentials through the contact form.
