Check compatibility before you order

What works depends on the reader configuration, the card product, the phone and the capabilities each device reports. The tables list what is documented; ask us to confirm anything else for your project.

Card programming

Supported SmartphoneKey reader configurations also let you program MIFARE Classic and MIFARE DESFire EV3 cards. A family name does not cover every memory size, credential format or card from another supplier. Reading a card, programming it and assigning access are separate operations.

Card programming
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
MIFARE Classic Program Programmed from the Admin Panel on a SmartphoneKey reader that supports programming. Available on supported reader configurations. Confirm the card's memory size and any credential format already on it before ordering. Reading a card's identifier is a different operation from programming it.
MIFARE DESFire EV3 Program Programmed from the Admin Panel on a SmartphoneKey reader that supports programming. Available on supported reader configurations. The documentation names MIFARE DESFire EV3 only; other DESFire generations and memory sizes need confirmation before they are planned. Manufacturer or standard information

Keys and cards

Wallet keys depend on the phone, its operating system and the account configuration. Do not assume a key works the same way on every phone when its battery is low or empty.

Keys and cards
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
Wallet keys Manage Issue and revoke Wallet keys (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet) for people and doors from the Admin Panel. Depends on the phone, its operating system and the account configuration. Do not assume the same behaviour when a phone's battery is low or empty.
NFC cards Manage Issue, review and revoke NFC cards in the Admin Panel alongside digital keys. A new card is programmed on a SmartphoneKey reader that supports programming, and must be MIFARE Classic or MIFARE DESFire EV3.

Matter devices

Support depends on capabilities. A device may report on/off, contact, temperature, lock, valve or other capabilities; what you can do with it depends on what it reports and what your configuration supports. Specific third-party models are listed only after compatibility testing.

Matter devices
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
Matter devices, by capability Control Devices that report supported capabilities, such as on/off, contact, temperature, lock or valve, can be commissioned and used in device management and scenarios. Available operations depend on what the device reports, its firmware and the configured system. Commissioning a device does not prove every command works. Specific third-party models are listed only after compatibility testing. Manufacturer or standard information
Scenario Builder Control Simple and advanced scenarios combine supported triggers, conditions and actions. Each current scenario belongs to one hub and its commissioned devices. An edited scenario replaces the version on the hub only once the hub has applied it.

Networks

Thread mesh routing, the Thread Border Router role, Matter control and the internet connection are separate roles. Every SmartphoneKey reader can act as a Thread Border Router and as a router in the mesh, without a separate firmware version. Matter controller functions are confirmed for each project, and the route to the internet is planned for each installation.

Networks
Family or model Operation What it covers Conditions and limits
LTE reader connection Connect Internet connection over cellular service. Needs cellular coverage at the reader position and a suitable SIM and service. No automatic switch to Wi-Fi is implied.
Wi-Fi reader connection Connect Internet connection over the site's Wi-Fi network. Needs suitable Wi-Fi coverage at the reader position and the network configuration given in the installation guide.
Reader through a Matter hub Connect Supported reader configurations can connect through a Matter hub. Depends on the reader model and firmware. This is not a radio-range or failover guarantee.
Reader Thread roles Connect Every SmartphoneKey reader can act as a Thread Border Router and as a router in the Thread mesh. No separate firmware version is needed for either role. Mesh coverage and the route to the internet depend on placement, power and the building. Matter controller functions are confirmed for each project.

Questions

Which cards can SmartphoneKey program?

Supported SmartphoneKey reader configurations can program MIFARE Classic and MIFARE DESFire EV3 cards. Confirm the exact card product, memory size and any existing credential format before ordering.

Can I use a third-party card programmer?

No. Cards are programmed with a SmartphoneKey reader that supports programming; SmartphoneKey does not support third-party card programmers.

Does SmartphoneKey work with every Matter device?

It depends on the device type, the capabilities it reports, its firmware and your configuration. Check the functions your scenario needs rather than assuming every Matter device offers them.
