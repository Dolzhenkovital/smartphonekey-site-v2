Oleksii Andrieiev

Architecte de solutions embarquées et cloud

Oleksii Andrieiev brings over 20 years of expertise in embedded engineering, industrial automation, and enterprise software. He designs and implements integrated systems that securely connect physical devices with cloud infrastructures, enabling applications such as mobile credentials and intelligent access systems.

At Smartphonekey, Oleksii leads the architecture of integrated solutions thatspan embedded firmware, communication interfaces, and scalable cloud services.His work enables secure, real-time interaction between hardware devices and digital platforms—supporting applications such as mobile credentials, digital identity, and intelligent access systems.

Previously, Oleksii held senior roles at Oracle and JFrog, specializing in continuous integration, deployment, and infrastructure automation. He holds a Master’s degree in Process Automation and Remote Device Management and multiple certifications in cloud and DevOps.
