Introduction to SmartphoneKey

Welcome to SmartphoneKey, where we offer a smart and cost-efficient access management system designed for multifamily properties. Our mission is to revolutionize access control, providing a secure, convenient, and app-free solution that meets the needs of tenants, property managers, and Airbnb hosts.

Our Mission at SmartphoneKey

At SmartphoneKey, our goal is to simplify access management for everyone. Whether you are a resident looking for a secure and easy way to enter your home, a property manager seeking to enhance building security, or an Airbnb host aiming to provide guests with a smooth check-in experience, SmartphoneKey is designed to meet your needs.

Why Choose SmartphoneKey?

SmartphoneKey is more than just an access control system. It’s a comprehensive solution that offers multiple benefits:

  • For Residents and Tenants: Enjoy seamless, app-free access using secure key cards or by adding SmartphoneKey to your Apple or Google wallets. Even if your phone’s battery is dead, you can still gain entry with the key card.
  • For Property Managers: Enhance building security and simplify access management with easy, wireless installation. Our user-friendly admin interface makes managing resident and guest access effortless.
  • For Airbnb Hosts: Provide guests with convenient, keyless entry using secure digital keys. Streamline the check-in process and improve guest experience without the need for physical key exchanges.

How SmartphoneKey Works

  1. Get SmartphoneKey: Install the device at the entry point of your property for wireless, seamless integration.
  2. Receive the Key Card: Use the secure, non-copyable key card to unlock doors with one touch.
  3. Add to the Wallet: Add the key card to Apple or Google Wallet for direct phone access.
  4. Open the Door: Use the phone to unlock the door, even if the phone's battery is dead.

Key Features of SmartphoneKey

  • Key Card Access: Unlock doors using a non-copyable key card, providing top-level security without the risk of duplication.
  • Wallet Integration: Add SmartphoneKey to your Apple or Google Wallet for convenient access. Unlock your property with just a tap, even if your phone’s battery is dead.
  • App-Free Operation: Enjoy the simplicity of accessing your property without needing a mobile app. SmartphoneKey's intuitive design ensures you always have access when you need it.
  • Temporary Key Sharing: Provide guests with temporary keys through a secure and straightforward process, making guest access easy and convenient.
  • Admin Interface: Manage access among residents and guests using a user-friendly chatbot. This admin interface simplifies control and monitoring of entry permissions.
  • QR Code Functionality: Scan QR codes to call specific apartments, offering a modern alternative to traditional intercom systems.

Looking Ahead with SmartphoneKey

We are committed to continuously improving SmartphoneKey to meet the evolving needs of our users. Our vision includes introducing new features, enhancing existing ones, and integrating with more smart home devices. Stay tuned for updates and innovations as we strive to provide the best access control solutions on the market.
