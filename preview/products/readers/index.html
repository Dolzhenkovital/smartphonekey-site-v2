Reader and access controller in one device

SmartphoneKey readers read NFC cards and digital keys, and the access controller is built in. Choose the LTE or Wi-Fi version according to the connection at each door.

Compare LTE and Wi-Fi Installation and connection guide

What the reader does

The reader reads NFC cards and Wallet keys and contains the access controller for the door. It connects to SmartphoneKey over LTE or Wi-Fi to receive changes and report events.

What else the installation needs

  • Power at each door
  • Compatible lock hardware, and any exit devices the door needs
  • Wiring as described in the installation and connection guide
  • A reliable LTE or Wi-Fi connection at the reader position

LTE or Wi-Fi

Both versions use the same Admin Panel, include the access controller, can act as a Thread Border Router and mesh router, and need power, compatible lock hardware and wiring. What differs is the connection:

Ask us which version suits your region. The connection is chosen door by door.

Every reader is a Thread router and Border Router

Each SmartphoneKey reader can act as a Thread Border Router and as a router in the Thread mesh. Neither role needs a separate firmware version, so the readers on a site can form one mesh and connect it to your IP network.

A suitable wireless design can reduce communication cabling between readers: readers on the Thread mesh can reach the internet through a reader that has an LTE or Wi-Fi connection.

Example connection layout

  1. Reader at the main entrance LTE or Wi-Fi, connected to the internet
  2. Reader at the side door Thread mesh
  3. Reader at the bike store Thread mesh

An example, not a specification. Coverage, the number of readers and the route to the internet depend on the installation.

Mesh coverage and the route to the internet still depend on placement, power and the building. How many readers share one connection, and what happens if it drops, depend on the installation; automatic failover is not implied. Matter controller functions are confirmed for each project.

Coverage depends on the building, placement and radio conditions. We help plan the network for your site.

Questions

How do the LTE and Wi-Fi readers differ?

They connect differently. The LTE reader uses a cellular network and needs a SIM and service; the Wi-Fi reader uses the site's Wi-Fi and needs reliable coverage and network settings. Check the connection at each door position.

Can one reader provide connectivity for other readers?

Yes. Every reader can act as a Thread router and as a Border Router, so readers can share a Thread mesh and reach the internet through a reader that has a connection. How many readers share that connection, and what happens if it drops, depend on the installation; we plan the topology with you. Automatic failover is not implied.

How far apart can readers be installed?

Coverage depends on the building, obstacles, placement and hardware configuration. Plan and check coverage on site; no single distance fits every installation.

Can I keep existing locks or cameras?

Check the exact lock hardware, its electrical interface and the camera models first. Send us the model details before you plan to reuse an existing installation.
