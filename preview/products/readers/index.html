Questions

How do LTE and Wi-Fi readers differ?

They provide different internet connection options. LTE requires suitable cellular service; Wi-Fi requires suitable network coverage and configuration. Confirm the model and installation requirements for your site.

Can one reader provide connectivity for other readers?

Supported hub and reader configurations can use a shared network path. The exact uplink, Thread roles and topology must be confirmed for the installed hardware and firmware. Do not assume every reader is a Border Router or provides failover.

How far apart can readers be installed?

Coverage depends on the radio environment, obstacles, placement and hardware configuration. Plan and verify coverage at the site; a universal distance cannot describe every installation.

Can I keep existing locks or cameras?

Compatibility must be checked against the exact hardware, electrical interface and camera or service requirements. Send model details to the team before assuming an existing installation can be reused.
