Vadym Sydorenko

Засновник і генеральний директор

Vadym Sydorenko is the driving force behind SmartphoneKey, leveraging over two decades of experience in the tech and telecom industries. He started his journey as a tech-savvy student, installing internet access in dorms and quickly growing into leadership roles, including CEO of one of Ukraine’s largest ISPs. His passion for technology and problem-solving led him to co-found OMO Systems, where he developed IoT solutions tailored for internet providers.

With a vision to simplify smart access control, Vadym founded SmartphoneKey, focusing on innovative solutions that make property access easy and secure for everyone. He is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life through technology, bringing his expertise in IoT and smart home devices to the forefront of the Fourth Industrial Revolution.
