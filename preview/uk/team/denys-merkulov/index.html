Технічний директор

Denys Merkulov brings extensive expertise in overseeing technical strategies and innovation within the telecom and technology sectors. In his role as CTO at SmartphoneKey, Denys drives the technological vision, ensuring the integration of cutting-edge solutions across product development, manufacturing processes, and logistics operations. He is committed to enhancing operational efficiency, scalability, and quality assurance, laying the foundation for sustainable growth and technological excellence. Denys continuously seeks innovative methods to elevate operational performance, solidifying SmartphoneKey's position as a leader in smart access technology.
