Kostiantyn Dolzhenko

Генеральний директор телекомунікаційного оператора

Kostiantyn Dolzhenko serves as an Advisor at SmartphoneKey, bringing a wealth of expertise from the telecom sector. As the CEO of Inalan, a prominent telecom provider in Greece, Kostiantyn has played a crucial role in advancing broadband connectivity and driving technological innovation across the region. Under his leadership, Inalan has become a leading force in providing high-speed internet solutions and fostering digital growth.

With a strong background in telecom management and strategic development, Kostiantyn has overseen numerous initiatives aimed at expanding network infrastructure and enhancing service quality. His career is marked by a commitment to delivering cutting-edge telecom solutions and improving digital access for consumers.

Kostiantyn is recognized for his strategic acumen and dedication to transforming the telecom landscape. His contributions continue to shape the future of connectivity in Greece and beyond, making him a valuable advisor to SmartphoneKey.
