Check compatibility

Choosing the connection

Whichever reader you choose, it needs power, compatible lock hardware and a reliable connection at the door.

Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi reader, if there is reliable Wi-Fi coverage at every door. Check coverage at each reader position before ordering.
Cellular (LTE)
LTE reader, where cellular coverage at the door is reliable and you can provide a SIM and service. Check coverage at each reader position, not just outside the building.
Both or not sure
Check Wi-Fi coverage at each door first. Choose a Wi-Fi reader where it is reliable and an LTE reader where only cellular coverage is reliable; if neither is, ask us for a site review.
