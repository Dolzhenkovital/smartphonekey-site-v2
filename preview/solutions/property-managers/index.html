Access for residents, staff and contractors

Give access when a resident moves in and revoke it when they move out. Give staff the doors they need, give contractors access for the job, and see which buildings need attention.

Site recommendations Discuss your properties

Everyday operations

Move-in and move-out

Add the new resident to their site and issue a Wallet key or NFC card. At move-out, revoke it and check that each reader has applied the change; a revocation waiting for an offline reader is not yet in effect there.

Staff and contractors

Give staff the doors their work needs. Give a contractor access for the job, temporary where supported, and revoke it when the work ends.

Site administrators

Let a local administrator manage access for their building, while you keep one way of working across every site.

Every building at a glance

Review events and reader status for each site, see changes that are still pending and configure alerts for supported device events.

Cameras and connected devices

Add supported cameras and device scenarios where they solve a real need. Check camera models and service scope before you plan them.

What to prepare

Bring these details to the site recommendations or to a conversation with us:

  • Number of sites, and doors at each site
  • Resident, staff and contractor groups, and the doors each group needs
  • Existing lock hardware at each door
  • Wi-Fi or cellular coverage at each door
  • Cards, Wallet keys or both
  • How visitors should reach residents
  • Who issues and revokes access day to day, and who handles support
