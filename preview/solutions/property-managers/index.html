Keep access organised across your buildings.

Residents change. Staff need different doors. Contractors arrive for a limited job. SmartphoneKey gives property teams a structured place to manage those changes.

Day-to-day operations

Move-in and move-out.

Put the person in the correct site, issue the credentials they need and revoke access when their permission ends. Check device status for changes that are waiting to be delivered.

Local responsibility.

Let the appropriate local administrator handle the site while maintaining a consistent operating process across the portfolio.

Service work.

Manage supported guest or temporary credentials for the required doors. Review the outcome and close the access lifecycle after the visit.

Connected operations.

Add supported cameras and device workflows where they solve a real operational need. Check model compatibility and service scope before deployment.

Project checklist.

Bring these details to the setup guide or to a conversation with our team:

  • Number of sites and doors
  • Resident, staff and contractor groups
  • Existing lock hardware
  • Connectivity at each door
  • Desired credentials
  • Visitor workflow
  • Support responsibilities
