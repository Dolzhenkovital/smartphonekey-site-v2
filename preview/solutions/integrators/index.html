Build the installation. Keep the customer relationship.

Use SmartphoneKey to organise customer sites and support the access system after installation. Combine the right reader configuration with clear handover and management workflows.

From evaluation to support.

  1. Evaluate.

    Check door hardware, power, network conditions, credential requirements and supported devices.

  2. Install.

    Follow the model-specific installation guidance. Choose LTE or Wi-Fi according to coverage and the site's network requirements.

  3. Hand over.

    Organise the site, configure supported access and assign the appropriate local management responsibilities.

  4. Support.

    Find the customer's site, review its devices and use activity and operation status to investigate issues.

Choose the reader configuration.

LTE and Wi-Fi readers share the same platform. The connection the site can support decides which one fits; power and compatible door hardware are needed either way. Every reader can also act as a Thread Border Router and as a router in the Thread mesh, with the same firmware.
