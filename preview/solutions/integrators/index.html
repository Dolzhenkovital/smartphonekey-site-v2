Install, hand over and support access systems

Assess each door, install SmartphoneKey readers, set up the customer's sites and administrators, and support the installation after handover.

Become a partner Installation and connection guide

From assessment to support

  1. Assess

    Check door hardware, power, Wi-Fi or cellular coverage at each door, the cards or digital keys the customer wants and any connected devices.

  2. Install

    Follow the installation and connection guide for each reader. Choose LTE or Wi-Fi for each door according to coverage and the site's network rules.

  3. Hand over

    Set up the customer's sites, doors and access, and assign their site administrators.

  4. Support

    Find the customer's site, review its readers, events and the status of each change, and investigate issues with the customer.

Choose the reader for each door

LTE and Wi-Fi readers work with the same platform. Choose by the connection each door position can support; both need power and compatible lock hardware. Every reader can also act as a Thread Border Router and as a router in the Thread mesh, without a separate firmware version.

LTE or Wi-Fi: what fits your site? Installation and connection guide

Your role and the customer's

The customer's administrators manage their own people, keys and cards. You can be given access to the customer's sites to commission readers and support them after handover; what you can see and do depends on the permissions granted to you.
