Ioannis Mavridis

CEO of Telecom Provider

Ioannis Mavridis serves as an Advisor at SmartphoneKey, bringing a wealth of experience from the telecommunications sector. As the CEO of Cablenet, a leading telecom provider in Cyprus, Ioannis has been pivotal in advancing telecom infrastructure and driving innovation in the industry. His leadership has guided Cablenet through significant growth, establishing it as a top player in the market known for its high-quality services and technological advancements.

With a career spanning over two decades, Ioannis has been at the forefront of telecom technology, focusing on expanding connectivity solutions and enhancing customer experiences. His expertise includes strategic planning, market expansion, and implementing cutting-edge telecom solutions that meet evolving customer needs.

Ioannis is recognized for his strategic vision and dedication to improving telecommunications services. His insights and leadership continue to shape the future of connectivity in Cyprus and beyond.
